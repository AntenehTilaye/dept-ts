# Department Management Tool Suite — Implementation Plan

## Context

An academic faculty needs one web platform for the day-to-day running of its departments: committees and tasks, course portfolios with assessment results and CQI, staff evaluation, add/drop and elective demand collection, instructor course preferences, invigilation and lab scheduling, resources, appointments and follow-up cases, meetings and minutes, annual plans and quarterly reports, plus notifications, templates, documents, reporting, search and audit. Today this is done with paper, email and spreadsheets; the goal is a single source of truth that lets a Department Head answer "what is active, what is overdue, who has submitted, what changed since last semester" without chasing people.

The requirements document (34 sections) is the authoritative spec. Its governing design rule (§33) is that the suite must **not** be 15–20 independent CRUD modules: Tasks, Forms/Campaigns, Workflow, Notifications, Templates, People and Documents are reusable platform services, and every feature module is a thin composition over them.

## Plan conventions (user, 2026-09-17)

- The plan is **phase by phase**. Every phase lists: goal, deliverables (files and modules), the tests written in that phase, the exact verification commands, and the **commit** that closes it. No phase is "done" until its tests pass and the commit is made.
- Phase 0 starts with `git init` (the directory is not a repository), a `.gitignore`, and the first commit of the scaffold.
- Test layers used throughout: **unit** (Vitest, pure logic: workflow engine transitions, form validation, import validators, metric calculations, reminder scheduling), **integration** (Vitest against a real PostgreSQL in Docker: Prisma services, tenancy scoping, jobs), **end-to-end** (Playwright against the running app: the user journeys of each phase). Each phase adds to all three where applicable.
- Commits use conventional-commit style messages (`feat(workflow): …`, `test(campaign): …`) and end with the attribution line required by this session.

## Architecture (from the 3-architect / 2-judge / critic-repaired blueprint, 2026-09-17)

The suite is a **platform kernel plus thin feature modules**. The kernel has three layers, and the admin feature builder (next section) is what makes the engine layer composable from a form.

**Foundation layer (canonical registry, referenced by foreign key everywhere)**
- Identity & Access: better-auth users; `Role`/`Permission`/`RolePermission` seeded from the §30 matrix and editable; `RoleGrant` with scope (`global|department|committee|section|program|section_offering|lab_schedule|meeting`), validity, and `source manual|derived`. Derived grants are synced from registry facts by outbox handlers (committee membership → `COMMITTEE_MEMBER@committee`, section rep → `STUDENT_REP@section`, teaching assignment → `INSTRUCTOR@section_offering`, lab duty → `LAB_STAFF@lab_schedule`) and repaired by a nightly `grant_reconcile` job. One policy decision point `can(actor, permissionKey, subjectRef)` with defined level semantics (full, manage, review, own, assigned, participate, limited, view, submit).
- People & Org: `Department` (tenant), `Person` (everyone, with or without a login), `StaffProfile` + `ProfileItem`, `Student`, `Program`, `Section` (program × year × academic year), time-varying `StudentSectionMembership` and `SectionRepresentative`, and one `Group`/`GroupMembership` abstraction for committees, sections, meeting participants, panels, lab teams and ad-hoc audience snapshots; `resolveAudience(spec)`.
- Academic Registry & Calendar: `AcademicYear` (quarter boundaries), `Term`, typed `CalendarPeriod` (registration, add/drop, preference, elective, teaching, examination, portfolio submission, evaluation), `Course` (with `predecessorCourseId` lineage), `CourseOffering` (course × term, owns the `AssessmentScheme`), `SectionOffering` (offering × section, per-section assessment lock), one `TeachingAssignment` table (source manual/load import/preference review), `Enrollment`, `ClassTimetableSlot`, and one `Resource` table for rooms and labs; `resolveAnchor({periodKind, edge, offsetDays})` turns calendar periods into deadlines.

**Engine layer (polymorphic services; no module may re-implement these)**
- `SubjectRegistry` contract: every subject type registers `label`, `snapshot`, `contextOf`, `relationships(id, person)`, `variables`, `indexDoc`, `url`, `accessResolver`, so every service works on a `SubjectRef {subjectType, subjectId}`.
- Workflow Engine: data-defined, versioned `WorkflowDefinition` (states with categories, transitions with `requiredPermission`, guards by registered name, required fields/comment, effects: notify, emit, createTask, setField, invokeHandler, registerBlocks, scheduleAutoTransition); `WorkflowInstance` is the **only** owner of lifecycle state; subject rows hold derived timestamp caches only; `WorkflowTransitionLog`.
- Work Item: one `Task` table for every task-like thing (`kind` general | committee_task | department_task | action_item | case | student_issue | planned_activity | cqi_action | maintenance | lab_activity), `TaskAssignment` (person, group, or audience snapshotted into an ad-hoc group), `RecurrenceRule`, extensions `Case` and `PlannedActivity` keyed by `taskId`; deliverables are `DocumentLink(linkRole=deliverable, slotKey)`; acknowledgement is the assignment `Notification`.
- Form/Campaign Engine: versioned `FormDefinition`/`Question` (stable keys; types likert, scale, choice, number, date, text, ranked_list, course/person/task pickers, file, repeating_group; `sourceBinding` for dynamic options), `Campaign` (kind evaluation | add_drop | elective | preference | survey; window anchored to a calendar period; `anonymityMode`; audience; aggregation spec), `CampaignSubject`, `CampaignInvitation` (hashed one-time token), `Submission`/`Answer` (normalised rows only; `campaignId` nullable so committee reports, portfolio and CQI narratives, quarterly narratives, appointment requests and section-issue intake are **standalone submissions** bound to a workflow), `AggregationResult` with k-anonymity suppression. Structural anonymity: no FK path from invitation to submission, day-truncated timestamps, coarse cohort attributes.
- Scheduler & Notification: `ReminderSchedule` (offsets −7/−3/−1/0/+1 with channels and escalation), `ReminderSubscription` (deadline or interval kind, calendar-anchored), `ScheduledJob` (idempotency key `{subjectRef}:{schedule}:{offset}`), `Notification` (per-user inbox; the single acknowledge/decline mechanism for tasks, duties and announcements), `NotificationDelivery` per channel, `ChannelPreference`; `upcoming(person, range)` is the one feed for "what is next".
- Template & Rendering: versioned `Template`/`TemplateVersion` (kinds message, reminder, document, report, export_layout; channel variants; declared variables validated on save), variables resolved via `SubjectRegistry.variables`.
- Document: `Document`/`DocumentVersion` (immutable, checksum, lockable by an approving transition)/`DocumentLink` (polymorphic, link roles attachment, deliverable, tor, minutes, evidence, generated_output)/`DocumentAccessGrant`; access inherits from linked subjects.
- Comment/Thread: one `Thread`/`Comment` service for task comments, revision notes, case notes and DH↔rep conversations; `escalate` creates a `case` task.
- Audit & Domain Events: `AuditEvent` (field-level before/after) via a Prisma extension, `DomainEvent` transactional outbox drained by the worker.
- Tabular Import: `ImportBatch`/`ImportRow`/`ColumnMappingProfile` staged pipeline (parse → map → validate → preview → fix → commit) with registered validators/committers per kind (assessment, attendance, roster, students, staff, lab_schedule, class_timetable, exam_timetable, load_result, assets).
- Availability & Conflict: `AvailabilityPolicy` (appointments, invigilation, leave) and one `AvailabilityBlock` ledger (person or resource, hard/soft severity) read by every conflict check; Postgres exclusion constraint for hard blocks.
- Reporting & Export: `ReportDefinition` registry (PDF/XLSX/CSV/HTML via one HTML template), `GeneratedReport` stored as a Document, admin-editable versioned `ExportFormatSpec` with golden-sample validation for the load assigner.
- Search (`SearchIndexEntry` with ACL tokens, tsvector) and Dashboard read models (`DashboardProjection` for open issues, staff activity, portfolio status, campaign progress).

**Feature modules (thin adapters)**: committees (= Group + tasks + a `committee_report` standalone form under a workflow), portfolios/assessment/CQI (`Portfolio` + `PortfolioScope`, `AssessmentScheme/Component`, `AssessmentRecord`, `StudentCourseResult`, `CourseMetricsSnapshot`, `CQIReport`/`CQIItem` chain), evaluations/add-drop/electives/preferences (campaign kind adapters), annual plan/quarterly (`AnnualPlan`, `PlannedActivity` task extension, `QuarterlyReport`), appointments (`Appointment` + `appointment_request` submission + availability), cases (task extension), meetings (`Meeting`, `AgendaItem`, `Decision`, `MeetingAttendance`, action-item tasks, minutes template → locked Document version), invigilation and labs (`ExamSchedule/ExamSession`, `LabSchedule/LabSession`, one `DutyAssignment` table under a `schedule_publication` workflow), resources (`Asset`, `MaintenanceRecord`), load bridge (`ReviewedPreference`, `LoadCycle`, `LoadExport`), student communication (`Announcement`, threads, `student_issue` tasks).

**Workflow definitions seeded**: task, case, planned_activity, portfolio, committee_report, campaign, appointment, annual_plan, quarterly_report, meeting, schedule_publication, load_cycle, import_batch, course_offering (states and transitions as in the blueprint).

## Feature builder: admin-composable features (the centre of the design)

`src/platform/feature/` is a kernel service beside Workflow, Forms, Work Item, Scheduler, Template, Document and Identity. It never re-implements them: a **FeatureDefinition** (Zod-validated JSON, `src/platform/feature/schema.ts`) is **compiled at publish time** into their artefacts, and one **generic runtime** renders any definition.

**Definition aggregate** (`FeatureDefinitionSchema`): `key`, `name`, `labels`; `navigation {group operations|academic|people|communication|planning|resources|admin, order, icon, visibleRoles, showInDashboard}`; `scope {level faculty|department|program|section, scopeFrom department|parent|record_field}`; optional `parentSubject {subjectType, featureKey?, relation, required, inheritPermissions, listUnderParent, createFromParent, allowedTypes?}`; `record {numberPrefix, titleTemplate (mustache), fields[], canCreate[], ownerRule, backing: feature_record | task{taskKind, extension?} | module{adapter}}`; `presets{}` (kind presets: locked field defaults, per-step form overrides, adapter overrides, `permissionPrefix`, per-preset parent type and nav label); `steps[]` = an ordered **tree** of `StepDef | StepGroup (sequential sub-steps) | ParallelGroup {branches static[2+] | dynamic perPerson, completion all|quorum(n)|any, onComplete, onAnyReject}`; each `StepDef` has `stepType form|review|upload|adapter|wait`, an inline form section or `formKey`, attachment slots, `assignee` rule, `actions[]` (`kind submit|approve|reject|request_revision|complete|cancel|reopen|assign|custom|auto`, `to` = step | group | terminal | `$next` | `$self`, `actors[]`, requiredComment/Fields/Attachments, guards, effects, explicit `permission`, `auto` trigger deadline|field_datetime|after_days|event), `deadline` rule (fixed | relative | calendar anchor), `reminders {scheduleKey, audience, escalateToRole}`, `notifications {onEnter[], onExit[]}`, `canView`, `canEdit`, code `adapter {onEnter,onExit,compute,validate}` and `surface` (both implicitly locked), `workItem {createTask, priority}`; `terminalStates[]`; `listViews[]`; `dashboardCounters[]`; optional `report`; `permissions.defaults` per role; `lockedPaths[]` (RFC 6901 pointers). Actor/assignee rules: owner, creator, assignee, role (department or parent scope), relationship (parent_owner, parent_chair, parent_member, requester, reviewer, participant, section_rep, teaching_staff_of_parent), permission key, person, group, record_field, system.

**Storage** (`prisma/schema/feature.prisma`): `FeatureDefinition` (key, nullable `departmentId` = faculty-wide; a department row with the same key shadows it; `isSystem`, `activeVersionId`), `FeatureDefinitionVersion` (`json`, `compiledJson`, `jsonHash`, `lockedHash`, `changeNote` `seed:<hash>` | `seed-upgrade:<hash>` | admin text, `workflowDefinitionId`), `FeatureRecord` (number `F-<prefix>-<year>-<seq>`, `data` jsonb, preset, parent subject, scope, owner, `workflowInstanceId` unique, derived caches `currentStateKey`/`branchStatesCache`/`deadlineAt`/`closedAt` written only by `feature.enterStep/exitStep/setTerminal` effects, `taskId` for task-backed features), `FeatureStepInstance` (step, group, branch or personId, sequence, status pending|active|done|skipped|rejected, assignee, `taskId`, `submissionId`, deadline), `FeatureNumberSequence`, `FeatureMigration`, `AdapterRegistration`. **The FeatureRecord is always the workflow subject**; module rows (`Portfolio`, `Meeting`, `Campaign`, `Committee`, …) carry `featureRecordId @unique` and never a workflow instance of their own. Every active step of a feature-record- or module-backed feature is one `Task(kind=feature_step)` so it shows in My Work and the inbox; task-backed features (task, case, student_issue, planned_activity) *are* the Task row.

**Compile at publish** (`compile.ts`, pure, snapshot-tested): normalise → flatten groups (groups add ordering and `$next` scope only) → resolve `$next` → states (leaf steps; each `ParallelGroup` becomes one **compound** workflow state with `BranchSpec[]`, synthetic `<group>.<branch>.$done/$rejected`, completion rule; dynamic groups use one `$person` branch template) → transitions (`<step>.<action>`, guards `feature.actorAllowed` + `feature.stepComplete` + custom, effects exit/notify/custom/enter-next|enterParallel|setTerminal|completeBranch|rejectBranch; synthetic `<group>.$join` and `<group>.$reject`) → auto actions (`scheduleAutoTransition` jobs or outbox subscriptions with `EventHandlerReceipt` idempotency) → FormDefinitions per step (new version only when the question hash changes) → TaskTemplates per step → ReminderTemplates → Permission/RolePermission rows (`feature.<key>.view|create|manage|act.<action>` or `<presetPrefix>.<action>`) and **grantRequirements** (which derived RoleGrants the actor rules rely on) → ReportDefinition. `publishVersion()` does validate → compile → upsert artefacts → activate in one transaction, then emits `feature.published` (nav cache bust, adapter refresh, search).

**Validation** (`validate.ts`): the fixed rule-code list KEY_FORMAT, KEY_UNIQUE, INITIAL_IS_STEP, TO_RESOLVES, NEXT_UNRESOLVED, REACHABLE, TERMINAL_EXISTS, DEAD_END, ACTOR_PRESENT, ASSIGNEE_RESOLVABLE, GRANT_SOURCE, PARALLEL_BRANCHES, PARALLEL_EXIT, REVISION_LOOP, REQUIRED_FIELDS, REQUIRED_ATTACHMENTS, FORM_REF, BINDING_ARGS, TEMPLATE_EXISTS, SCHEDULE_EXISTS, DEADLINE_FIELD, ROLE_EXISTS, PARENT_TYPE, SCOPE_CONSISTENT, ADAPTER_EXISTS, SURFACE_EXISTS, PRESET_CONSISTENT, LIST_COLUMNS, LOCK_VIOLATION, BACKING_LOCKED, WARN_NO_DEADLINE, WARN_NO_NOTIFY; one failing fixture per code in `tests/unit/feature/validate.test.ts`. **Simulate** (`simulate.ts`): runs the compiled machine in memory with a scripted actor and path, reporting states, branch states, tasks, deadlines, reminders, notifications, guards and grants needed; also a CLI (`scripts/feature-simulate.ts`).

**Versioning and migration**: records pin `definitionVersionId`; editing a published definition creates one DRAFT (N+1); publishing never touches running records; the migration wizard (`/admin/features/[key]/migrate`: diff → preview → run) maps old states to new ones or `$block`, runs per department as a resumable pg-boss job (`feature.migrate`, batches of 200), blocks records inside a compound state unless every branch is mapped, and logs a `migrate` transition per record.

**Admin wizard** (`/admin/features/new`, `[key]/edit`, `versions`, `simulate`, `migrate`, `records`), seven screens: Identity; Hierarchy (navigation placement, organizational scope, parent nesting); Record fields (field builder, presets); Steps (`StepTree` with sub-step groups and parallel groups, `StepDrawer` tabs Form / Attachments / Assignee / Actions & transitions with a transition graph / Deadline & reminders / Notifications / Who may view or edit; locked controls show a `LockBadge`); Terminals, lists and counters; Report; Validate, simulate and publish. `cloneDefinitionAction` starts from any published definition (`generic_request` is the sample).

**Generic runtime** (`src/app/(app)/d/[dept]/f/[featureKey]/`): list (`ListView`, counters, TanStack table with URL filters), `new` (RecordForm from `zodFromFields`, preset and parent pickers), `[recordId]` (header, `StepTimeline` with `ParallelLanes`, current step panels with `ActionBar`, attachment slots, documents, thread, audit), `[recordId]/steps/[stepKey]` (`FormRenderer` of the pinned form version, uploads via `/api/uploads`, or a registered `stepRenderers[stepKey]` surface), `history`, nested child lists, `report/[reportKey]`. Built-in URLs such as `/d/[dept]/committees/...` are **rewrites** to `/f/committee/...`; there is one implementation of every page. `getNav(actor, dept)` builds the sidebar from published definitions; `featureCounters()` is a grouped count over `(department_id, definition_id, current_state_key)`.

**Seeded built-ins** (`prisma/seed/features/<key>.ts`, typed `FeatureDefinition` objects; `seedFeatures()` publishes new ones, leaves admin-edited versions alone, and creates a `seed-upgrade` DRAFT when a locked part changed in code): `committee` (module-backed, Group-based; steps setup → active ⇄ inactive → dissolved), `committee_report` (parent committee; draft → submitted → reviewed → approved | revision_required), `task` (task-backed; presets for every TaskKind; draft → assigned → in_progress → submitted → under_review → completed | revision_required | cancelled), `case` (task-backed with Case extension; open → assigned → in_progress ⇄ waiting → resolved → closed, auto-close), `portfolio` (parent course_offering; sequential group prepare{course_info, assessment (surface), narrative, cqi} → ready → submitted → **parallel review {dh_review, dpt_review} quorum 1** → approved | revision_required), `cqi` (parent portfolio; comparison (adapter) → prior_items → narrative → complete, recompute on `assessment.committed`), `campaign` (**one** feature, presets add_drop | elective | preference | evaluation | survey with `permissionPrefix` so `evaluation.close/analyze` stay DH-only; setup → published → in_progress → closed → analyzed → archived), `appointment` (requested → confirmed ⇄ rescheduled → completed | no_show | rejected | cancelled; public intake at `/a/[hostSlug]`), `meeting` (scheduled → held → minutes_draft → **dynamic parallel circulation, one branch per participant, completion all, onAnyReject changes_requested** → approval → minutes_approved), `annual_plan`, `planned_activity` (task-backed; stored Delayed state), `quarterly_report`, `exam_schedule`, `lab_schedule`, `load_cycle`, `import_batch` (presets per import kind), `student_issue` (task-backed, section-scoped), `announcement`, `course_offering`, and the non-system sample `generic_request` (parallel reviewers quorum 1). Each lists its code-backed adapters (`<module>.backing`, guards, effects, computes, surfaces, exports) which are registered in `src/modules/<module>/adapters.ts` and rendered read-only in the wizard.

## Prisma schema, tenancy and auth (summary; the full model list is in the design journal)

- **Multi-file schema** `prisma/schema/*.prisma`: `schema.prisma` (generator `prisma-client`, `output ../../src/generated/prisma`, `moduleFormat esm`), `enums.prisma` (all enums incl. the merged `SubjectType`), `auth.prisma` (generated by `npx auth@latest generate`; only snake-case maps and two back-relations are hand-applied), then one file per service (`identity`, `people`, `academic`, `workflow`, `feature`, `workitem`, `forms`, `campaign`, `scheduler`, `notification`, `template`, `document`, `thread`, `audit`, `import`, `availability`, `reporting`, `search`, `dashboard`) and per module (`committee`, `portfolio`, `meeting`, `planning`, `load`, `scheduling`, `resource`). About 95 models.
- **Conventions**: text `cuid()` ids everywhere (better-auth ids are text); `Department.id === Organization.id`; every model and column `@@map/@map` snake_case (checked by `prisma/scripts/check-schema.ts`); every Json column has a Zod schema in `src/lib/db/json-schemas.ts`; `rowVersion` optimistic concurrency on edited rows; polymorphic `REF(x)` = `xType SubjectType` + `xId` + index, no FK.
- **Tenancy classes** (`prisma/rls-manifest.json`, generated and checked by `prisma/scripts/gen-rls.ts`): GLOBAL (auth tables, `department`, `permission`, `system_setting`, `person`, `channel_preference`, `event_handler_receipt`, `adapter_registration`, `report_definition`, `question`, `template_version`, `feature_definition_version`); SHARED-OR-TENANT (nullable `departmentId`, NULL = faculty-wide: `role`, `role_permission`, `workflow_definition`, `form_definition`, `reminder_schedule`, `template`, `column_mapping_profile`, `export_format_spec`, `feature_definition`, `scheduled_job`, `audit_event`, `domain_event`); TENANT (everything else, `departmentId NOT NULL`, `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, policy `department_id = current_setting('app.current_department_id', true) OR current_setting('app.tenant_bypass', true) = 'on'`). `Person` is global and linked to departments through `DepartmentPerson`; `Person.userId @unique` is the only link to a login.
- **Postgres roles** (only in `docker/postgres/init.sql`): `dept_migrator` (LOGIN, CREATEDB, BYPASSRLS, owner; used by `prisma migrate`, seeds, test resets via `DATABASE_URL_MIGRATE`) and `dept_app` (LOGIN, NOBYPASSRLS; web, worker, integration tests via `DATABASE_URL`). Databases `dept`, `dept_test`, `dept_e2e`; schema `pgboss` owned by `dept_app` and never touched by Prisma.
- **Hand-written migrations** (created with `migrate dev --create-only`): `extensions` (pg_trgm, btree_gist), `rls` + `rls_<phase>` (generated by `gen-rls.ts --append`), `constraints` (availability EXCLUDE on hard blocks, partial unique indexes, `task_backing_check`, anonymity trigger on `submission`, append-only REVOKEs + `raise_immutable` triggers for `audit_event`/`workflow_transition_log`/`event_handler_receipt`, column-guarded `domain_event`, `document_version` lock/purge via SECURITY DEFINER functions), `search` (tsvector generated column + GIN, trigram indexes).
- **Scoped access**: `src/lib/db/prisma.ts` → `prismaRoot`; `src/lib/db/scoped.ts` → `forDepartment(id)` query extension (injects `departmentId` into writes and wraps each operation in a transaction that sets `app.current_department_id`) and request-cached `getDb()`; `src/lib/db/tenant.ts` → `withTenantTx(departmentId, fn)` and audited `withTenantBypass(ctx, reason, fn)` (admin faculty pages, `grant.reconcile`, `retention.run`, seeds only). RLS is the boundary; the extension is a safety net.
- **better-auth** (`src/lib/auth/auth.ts`): `prismaAdapter(prismaRoot)`, `emailAndPassword {enabled, disableSignUp: true, requireEmailVerification: true, minPasswordLength: 10, sendResetPassword}`, plugins `admin({ adminRoles: ['admin'] })`, `organization({ ac, roles, allowUserToCreateOrganization: false, membershipLimit: 5000, organizationHooks → outbox member.changed/removed })`, `nextCookies()` **last**. `src/lib/auth/access.ts` defines the seven membership roles with `createAccessControl` (registered on server and `organizationClient`). Global System Administrator = `user.role === 'admin'`. `committee_chair` is a derived scoped `RoleGrant`, never a membership role. Users are provisioned by `identityService.provisionUser()` (createUser with a random password + `emailVerified: true`, link `Person.userId`, `addMember`, password-reset mail to `/set-password`). `Organization.slug` = lower-case department code = the `[dept]` URL segment; `requireDeptContext(slug)` treats the URL as the authority and re-syncs `session.activeOrganizationId`.
- **Permissions**: `can(actor, permissionKey, subjectRef?)` in `src/platform/identity/can.ts`: admin → allow; explicit `none` → deny; department-scope RolePermission (`full`; `manage` minus `SystemSetting rbac.manageExcludedPermissions` = portfolio.approve, evaluation.close, evaluation.analyze, plan.approve, minutes.approve); scoped grants matched through `SubjectRegistry.contextOf`; relationship levels (`review`, `own`, `assigned`, `participate`, `limited`, `view`, `submit`) evaluated live through `SubjectRegistry.relationships`. Workflow transitions require both the permission and the transition's actor rules. Derived grants are synced by outbox subscribers (member, committee membership/chair, teaching assignment, section rep with auto-invite, lab duty) and repaired nightly by `grant.reconcile`.
- **Persons without a login** (students, external requesters): email only, hashed one-time campaign tokens at `/c/[token]`, public appointment form at `/a/[hostSlug]` with a signed status link; representatives are auto-invited when assigned.

## Key risks and their mitigations (top items; the full list is in the design journal)

- RLS coverage drifting as tables are added → `gen-rls.ts` generates policies from the DMMF, `rls:check` and `rls-coverage.test.ts` fail the build on any uncovered tenant table.
- Compound (parallel) states under concurrent actions or during migration → `Workflow.apply` locks the instance with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`; the pure machine is exhaustively unit-tested; migrations block records inside a compound state unless every branch is mapped.
- Admin edits breaking code-backed built-ins → locked paths compared with the seed baseline, `LOCK_VIOLATION` at publish, adapter-existence checks, simulate before publish, records pin versions.
- Inner-platform effect → the runtime is proven on the seeded `task` feature before any module; modules only register adapters and surfaces; builder scope fixed to process features.
- Windows bind-mount performance and file-watching → named volumes, polling watchers, `docker compose watch` fallback, WSL2 clone as last resort.
- Anonymous evaluations de-anonymised → no FK path, trigger, day-truncated timestamps, coarse cohorts, k-anonymity suppression, adversarial join tests before the evaluation phase closes.
